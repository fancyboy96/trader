Overview
Screening produces a shortlist. Every Tier A output requires a manual fundamental report before any position is taken. The score surfaces candidates worth investigating. It does not replace analysis.
Pass / Fail Filters

Hard cutoffs applied before scoring. A ticker that fails any filter is excluded entirely and assigned Tier D regardless of other metrics.

FilterThresholdRationale
Market cap≥ $2BAvoid micro/small-cap liquidity risk

Free cash flow> 0Must be generating cash, not just accounting profit

Tiers
A
75–100 points
Strong candidate. Proceed to full fundamental report and consider a position.
B
50–74 points
Watch. Promising but a condition must be met first: a price pullback, a catalyst, or more data.
C
25–49 points
Weak. Pass unless there is a specific catalyst thesis not captured by the numbers.
D
0–24 points
Exclude. Does not meet the quality bar at current metrics.
Verdict language. Tier A → BUY (conviction entry, fundamentals support a position). Tier B → WATCH (promising, a condition must be met before entry). Tier C/D → PASS (valuation or quality does not support a position at current levels).
Data Source & Refresh

Fundamental data is pulled via yfinance and stored in a local SQLite database (data/trader.db). Prices cover a 2-year rolling window. Screening criteria are defined in planning/screening-criteria.md, the source of truth. screen.py implements it.
