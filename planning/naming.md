# Product Naming

**Chosen name: Pythia**

Pythia — the Oracle at Delphi. Pilgrims brought questions; she returned readings. The name frames the tool as a research instrument that takes a company and returns a considered verdict. Short, striking, no major naming conflicts in fintech.

---

## Shortlist

### Pythia ✓
The Oracle at Delphi. Pilgrims brought questions; she returned readings. Strong framing for a research tool that takes a company and returns a considered verdict. Short, striking, no major naming conflicts in fintech.

### Kairos
God of the opportune moment — the right opening at the right time. Relevant to the investment thesis: identifying not just good companies but good entry points. Implies timing and precision.

### Argo
The ship Jason sailed to find the Golden Fleece. Quest for wealth through bold exploration. Short and punchy. The Argonaut framing positions the user as the hero pursuing a valuable prize — but the name skews more toward action/adventure than analytical rigour.

### Aletheia
Goddess of truth and disclosure. Roman equivalent: Veritas. The disclosure/truth framing fits fundamental analysis well, but Pythia's oracle concept is sharper as a product identity.

---

## Rejected

### Prometheus
Literally "forethought" — strong concept. Already used by several AI and infrastructure projects, which dilutes it.

### Delphi
The oracle site. Clean and recognisable, but Delphi Technologies (auto parts) and Delphi (programming language) create noise.

### Cassandra
Perfect foresight, never believed. Darkly funny for a stock research tool. Wrong tone.

### Veritas
Roman equivalent of Aletheia. One syllable shorter, strong Latin gravitas. Ruled out because Veritas Technologies is a well-known enterprise software company.
